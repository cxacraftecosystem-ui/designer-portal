# Designer guide: Design Workshop

For designers running a design and prototype workshop. Use the website **designer-repository.vercel.app** or the **Design Workshop** app on an Android phone. Both show the same workshop.

## Get started

1. Sign in with the link the ministry sent you, or with Google or your email and password. The link works once and then expires.
2. Open **My designer profile** and complete it. It fills stages 1 and 3 and puts your name on the report.
3. Open **Design workshops**. Your workshop appears once an officer has named you on it.

## Fill in the stages

1. Open your workshop. It lists all 22 stages and shows how complete each one is.
2. Open a stage, fill in the boxes and press **Save stage**. **Save and check required fields** lists any required box that is still empty.
3. Each box has one line of help under it. Tap the **i** for the rest.
4. To speak instead of typing, press **Dictate** inside a long-answer box, then **Stop**. First record on the workshop's page whether the artisan agreed to be recorded.
5. Pick artisans, products, tools and processes from the lists instead of typing them again. Picking one copies its details into the stage. If an artisan is already recorded, the app says so.
6. A ring on each artisan shows how complete its record is. Open an artisan to print their **Artisan card**; on the phone, scan its QR code to pick them.
7. Add photos to each stage. If a photo is blurred or too small, the app turns it away, so take it again.

## Work without signal

1. Open the workshop once while you are online, so a copy is on your device.
2. Carry on as normal. Each save says **Saved on this device** and uploads by itself when you are back online.
3. On the phone, press **Sync now** in the workshop list to send your work straight away.
4. Do not uninstall the app or clear the browser's data while work is still waiting to upload.

## Hand in for review

1. Open **Readiness** to see what is still missing. You can hand in with empty boxes.
2. On the website, open the workshop and press **Hand in for inspection**. This needs a connection.
3. To make changes while officers are reading it, press **Withdraw from inspection**.

## Read the officers' notes

1. Notes appear on the workshop's page under **What the officers asked for**, round by round, each naming its stage.
2. If it says **Sent back**, fix the stages it names and save them. Saving a changed stage hands the workshop back in. If you fixed something outside the stages, press **Hand it back in**.
3. A note can point to a single box. When your workshop is sent back you get an e-mail and a notice under **Notifications**.

## Download the report

1. Open **Report**, choose the template and colour, then press **Download .docx** or **Download .pdf**.

## Turn on two-step verification

1. Go to **Settings** and switch on **Two-step verification**.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator. Type in the 6-digit code it shows and press **Turn on**.
3. Save your recovery codes somewhere safe. Each code works once, and you use one if you lose your phone.
4. From now on, every sign-in asks for a code. If you lose your phone and your codes, ask an admin to turn it off for you.
