# Officials guide: Design Workshop

For inspectors, approvers and the ministry desk. Use the website **designer-repository.vercel.app**. Inspectors can also use the **Design Workshop** Android app. The ministry desk screens are on the website only.

## Inspect a workshop

1. Open **Workshops to inspect**. You see only the workshops assigned to you. Search by title, craft, cluster or workshop code.
2. Open a workshop to read all 22 stages. Each answer shows who wrote it and when. You can read everything but change nothing.
3. To leave a note, type in **What should be corrected?** and choose the stage it is about, or **The report as a whole**.
4. Press **Suggest** to leave the report where it is, or **Send back** to return it to the designer as **Needs revision**.
5. A note cannot be changed once it is filed. If you change your mind, file another note.
6. Move between stages with the stage menu. A note can point to a single box. If a stage needs nothing, press **Checked, no corrections**.

## Approve a report and hand it on (Ministry Admin)

1. Open **Reports to approve** (four lists: **Waiting for approval**, **Approved, not yet handed on**, **Handed on**, **Returned**). Read a report stage by stage, with what the officers asked for.
2. Press **Approve**, adding a note if you want one. Or send it back, saying what needs correcting.
3. Press **Hand on to the office**, choose the office, add a note and attach the exported report. The office gets an e-mail with a link to download it.
4. An approved report is locked. To correct it, withdraw the approval and give a reason. You cannot decide on a report you worked on or inspected.

## Run the ministry desk

1. **Ministry dashboard** lists every workshop with its designer, standing and progress. Filter, search, or press **Refresh now**. **Download workshops** or **Download beneficiaries** saves a spreadsheet.
2. **Annual plan**: upload the year's plan spreadsheet. Upload the whole sheet each time. Press **Open workshop** to start a workshop from a row of the plan.
3. **Sanction orders**: record an order to open the workshop and invite its designer. The sign-in link is e-mailed to the designer. Press **Resend** if it did not arrive, and **Remind designers** to e-mail those who have not finished setting up.
4. **Workshop oversight**: choose a workshop and name its designers, the Assistant Director, the Regional Director and the inspector.
5. **Workshops I monitor** (Assistant and Regional Directors): read every stage of the workshops you supervise. You cannot edit them.
6. **Map**: switch to **Coverage** to see the districts and crafts the programme has and has not reached.
7. **Duplicate artisans** (admins): check pairs that look like the same person and merge them. A merge can be undone for 30 days.
8. Every Monday at 08:00 a **Weekly summary** says what waits for you, what is overdue and what came in, in the app and by e-mail. Switch it off in **Settings**.

## Review records

1. Open **Review** to see records waiting for a decision.
2. Choose **Approve**, **Reject** or **Send for revision**. Send for revision needs a comment saying what to change.
3. To approve several records at once, tick them and approve them together. Reject and send back one at a time.

## Turn on two-step verification

1. Go to **Settings** and switch on **Two-step verification**.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app, type in the 6-digit code, press **Turn on**, and save your recovery codes somewhere safe.
